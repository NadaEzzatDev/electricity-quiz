Hello there!

Here's how the lab works:

  • 🧩Tap a toolbar button to add a part — it wires itself into the circuit automatically.
  • ⚙️Tap any part in the circuit to tweak its value, connect it, or remove it.
  • 🎚️Drag the sliders to change voltage and resistance — the dashboard updates live.
  • 🔌Flip the switch to open or close the circuit and watch the current flow.
  • 🏆Try Quest Mode for level-by-level challenges, or stay in Sandbox Mode to experiment freely.
