# ⚡ Electricity Fundamentals Quiz — Student Guide

Welcome! This quiz will test what you know about **Voltage (V)**, **Current (I)**, **Resistance (R)**, **LEDs**, and **Ohm's Law**. It only takes a few minutes, and you can try again as many times as you like.

## How to open the quiz

Click this link (or ask your teacher for it if it has changed):

**👉 [https://nadaezzatdev.github.io/electricity-quiz/]**

It works on a phone, tablet, or computer — you just need an internet connection.

## How to take it

1. **Type your name** in the box on the first screen.
2. **Choose your grade** — Grade 7 or Grade 8.
3. Click **Start Quiz 🚀**.
4. You'll see 10 questions, one at a time. Click the answer you think is correct.
5. Right after you answer, you'll see whether you got it right, plus a short explanation — read it even if you got the question right, it helps the ideas stick!
6. Click **Next ➜** to move to the next question. On the last question, the button will say **See Results 🏁**.

## After you finish

You'll see your score out of 10, along with a review of every question — what you answered and what the correct answer was.

- If you get everything right, you'll see a special celebration! 🏆
- If you miss a few, don't worry — that's totally normal. Read the explanations, then hit **Try Again 🔄** to take the quiz again.

You can retake the quiz as many times as you'd like — there's no limit, and no penalty for trying again.

## A few tips

- Take your time — there's no timer, so read each question carefully.
- If you're not sure, think about the formulas: **Ohm's Law is V = I × R**.
- Getting a question wrong is part of learning — use the explanation to understand *why*, not just *what*.
- Made a mistake typing your name or picked the wrong grade? Click **New Student 👤** on the results screen to start fresh.

Good luck, and have fun — let's see how bright you shine! ⚡
