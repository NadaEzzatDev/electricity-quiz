# ⚡ Electricity Fundamentals Quiz — Student Guide

Welcome! This site will help you practice what you're learning about **Voltage (V)**, **Current (I)**, **Resistance (R)**, **LEDs**, and **Ohm's Law** — with a lab to build real circuits and a quiz to test yourself.

## How to open it

Click this link (or ask your teacher for it if it has changed):

**👉 https://nadaezzatdev.github.io/electricity-quiz/**

It works on a phone, tablet, or computer — you just need an internet connection. (For the Lab, a computer or laptop works best — it's easier to click and drag on a bigger screen.)

## Choose an activity

When the page opens, you'll see two options:

- 🧪 **Electricity Lab** — start here to practice! Build real circuits with batteries, resistors, LEDs, and switches. Drag sliders, flip switches, and watch the current flow. Try **Sandbox Mode** to experiment freely, or **Quest Mode** for level-by-level challenges.
- 📝 **Take the Quiz** — once you've practiced, test what you know with 10 quick questions. You get instant feedback and a score at the end.

**Tip: start with the Electricity Lab to get a feel for how circuits work, then take the quiz to check your understanding.**

## Using the Electricity Lab

1. Type your name when it asks, then read the quick tips on how the lab works.
2. Tap a toolbar button (Battery, Resistor, LED, Switch, Wire) to add a part — it wires itself into the circuit automatically.
3. Tap any part in the circuit to tweak its value, connect it, or remove it.
4. Drag the sliders to change voltage and resistance — the dashboard updates live.
5. Flip the switch to open or close the circuit and watch the current flow.
6. Try **Quest Mode** for level-by-level challenges, or stay in **Sandbox Mode** to experiment freely.

## Taking the quiz

1. **Type your name** in the box on the first screen.
2. **Choose your grade** — Grade 7 or Grade 8.
3. Click **Start Quiz 🚀**.
4. You'll see 10 questions, one at a time. Click the answer you think is correct.
5. Right after you answer, you'll see whether you got it right, plus a short explanation — read it even if you got the question right, it helps the ideas stick!
6. Click **Next ➜** to move to the next question. On the last question, the button will say **See Results 🏁**.

### After you finish

You'll see your score out of 10, along with a review of every question — what you answered and what the correct answer was.

- If you get everything right, you'll see a special celebration! 🏆
- If you miss a few, don't worry — that's totally normal. Read the explanations, then hit **Try Again 🔄** to take the quiz again.

You can retake the quiz as many times as you'd like — there's no limit, and no penalty for trying again.

## A few tips

- Take your time — there's no timer, so read each question carefully.
- If you're not sure, think about the formulas: **Ohm's Law is V = I × R**.
- Getting a question wrong is part of learning — use the explanation to understand *why*, not just *what*.
- Made a mistake typing your name or picked the wrong grade? Click **New Student 👤** on the results screen to start fresh.

Good luck, and have fun — let's see how bright you shine! ⚡
